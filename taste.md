# UX Principles

- Prioritize clarity over decoration.
- Use generous whitespace to improve readability.
- Highlight primary actions with strong contrast.
- Keep navigation simple and predictable.
- Reinforce trust with human photography and testimonials.
- Maintain consistent spacing, radii, and shadows.
- Use subtle motion to enhance, not distract.
- Ensure all interactive elements have clear hover and focus states.
- Design mobile-first with responsive scaling.
